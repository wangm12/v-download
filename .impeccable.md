## Design Context

### Users

People using V-Download to review and queue many online videos or creator posts, especially Douyin profile collections. They need to scan metadata quickly, select a precise subset, and add it to the download queue without losing their place.

### Brand Personality

Calm, dense, dependable. The interface should feel like a focused desktop media utility: fast and capable, but never noisy or intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark, editorial workspace with restrained contrast, compact media rows, and one clear action color. Prefer a structured panel over a generic centered modal. Use hierarchy, selection states, and spacing to make the workflow memorable rather than decorative.

### Design Principles

- Make selection progress and the next action continuously visible.
- Keep bulk controls grouped by intent: load, navigate, select, then queue.
- Use thumbnails as visual anchors while keeping titles and metadata scannable.
- Reserve the accent color for actionable or selected states.
- Preserve keyboard access, focus visibility, reduced motion, and compact performance for large post lists.
