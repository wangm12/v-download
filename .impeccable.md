## Design Context

### Users

People saving a single link or many creator posts, including people opening V-Download for the first time. They should be able to paste a link, choose content, and download without knowing about download engines, cookies, codecs, or templates.

### Brand Personality

Calm, clear, dependable. A focused desktop utility with familiar controls, readable lists, and one obvious next action.

### Aesthetic Direction

Adapt Raycast's black and white controls and Linear's gray surface hierarchy to a desktop app. Dark window #101114, sidebar #15161A, panel #1C1F25, selection #292D35, primary text #F4F4F6, secondary text #A4A9B4. Light window #FAFAFA, sidebar #F1F2F4, panel #FFFFFF, selection #E8EBEF, primary text #171717, secondary text #666A73. Dark primary buttons are white with dark text; light primary buttons reverse this.

Keep the established system font. Body copy is 13–14px, supporting copy 12px. Use the existing 8/10/12px button/card/panel radii. Keep existing semantic status colors in small dots, progress, and warnings with text or icons. Prefer grayscale hierarchy, restrained borders, and simple surfaces.

### Design Principles

- Keep the next action visible; chooser footers stay outside the scrolling content.
- Show information once. Hide zero remaining, completed progress, unavailable metrics, internal IDs, and codec details from the main scan path.
- Keep common actions visible and group occasional actions under More or a labeled disclosure.
- Preserve download behavior, pagination limits, default selections, recommendations, shortcuts, and saved settings.
- Translate all UI text into English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- Support keyboard focus, Escape and focus restoration, reduced motion, both themes, and small windows.
- Use one static icon across the app and Dock, with native progress and the existing task-count badge.
